ESP32 Network Monitor

Settings

Opens the NETMON_SETUP portal so you can reconfigure which network the device connects to. Join NETMON_SETUP from your phone/laptop, then browse to http://192.168.4.1:8080 to configure. The device will drop off this network until reconfigured.
